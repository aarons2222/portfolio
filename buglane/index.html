Quick answers

Getting started

How do I connect my workspace?

Enter the address you use to open Mantis in your browser, then sign in with your Mantis account or an API token. Buglane requires a secure HTTPS connection. If your organisation uses single sign-on, try an API token and ask your Mantis administrator for help if needed.

Why can't I see or change an issue?

Buglane uses the permissions granted by your Mantis workspace. Your administrator can help with your account, project access and the actions available to you. Issue changes also require an active Buglane Pro trial or Pro unlock.

What do the trial and Pro include?

Free access includes one active workspace and issue browsing. Where available, the optional 14-day trial unlocks Pro features, including issue changes, comments, uploads and multiple saved workspaces. There is no automatic charge when the trial ends. Pro is a one-time purchase; the app shows the current price for your App Store region.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings, choose Buglane Pro and select Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Your Mantis account and your Apple Account are separate.

How do notifications work?

When notifications are enabled, Buglane checks your workspace for changes and creates local alerts. Your device controls background refresh, so checks can be delayed or skipped. A notification can include an issue title, depending on your device settings.

What happens if I remove a workspace?

Removing a saved workspace removes its saved sign-in and local drafts from this device. It does not delete the Mantis account or issues on your server. Other saved workspaces and eligible App Store purchases remain available.
