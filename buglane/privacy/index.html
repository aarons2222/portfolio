Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Buglane is published by Aaron Strickland in the United Kingdom. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Buglane, visit these pages or contact support.

Buglane connects directly to the Mantis server you choose. It has no separate Buglane backend, advertising or analytics service. Apple handles App Store purchases.

Your Mantis workspace

Buglane handles your workspace address, account details, issues, comments, project information and files to provide the app. Information you submit is sent over HTTPS to your configured Mantis server. Your workspace operator controls the information held there, access to it and its own privacy practices. Contact your workspace administrator about access, correction or deletion of server-held information.

Information saved on your device

Passwords and API tokens are stored in your device's Keychain. Buglane also saves workspace data, drafts, selected report files and information about interrupted saves locally, so you can return to work and review uncertain results. Device backups may include app data according to your settings; saved credentials use device-only Keychain protection.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. When enabled, Buglane checks your Mantis workspace for changes and creates local notifications. Buglane does not operate a push-notification server. Alerts can show issue titles on your lock screen, depending on your device settings.

Purchases and free trials

Apple processes App Store purchases. Buglane checks Apple-verified purchase records on your device to enable Pro and its 14-day trial, and keeps a local time record in Keychain to preserve trial expiry. Purchases are associated with your Apple Account rather than your Mantis account. Buglane does not receive your payment-card details or send purchase records to a separate Buglane server. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and analytics

Buglane contains no advertising, tracking SDKs or analytics service. It does not send your Mantis credentials or issue data to a separate Buglane service.

Files and links

Buglane accesses files you select for upload and downloads files you open from Mantis. Files or issue links you export or share are handled by the destination you choose. External websites have their own privacy practices.

Removing information

Signing out or removing a saved workspace removes its saved connection and credentials from Buglane. Its local workspace cache and drafts are removed when no other saved connection uses the same Mantis account. Other saved workspaces and App Store purchases stay available. Files you exported elsewhere remain there. Removing a workspace does not delete your Mantis account or information on the server.

Support and these pages

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are used to reply and investigate your request. Please avoid sending passwords, API tokens, payment details or sensitive issue information. You can ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence using the contact below.

These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or externally hosted fonts. Vercel hosts them and may process ordinary request information such as IP address and browser information to deliver and protect the site. See Vercel's Privacy Notice.

Your choices and contact

You can manage notifications in device settings and remove locally saved workspace information as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its use, or request a copy. For questions or requests about Buglane or support correspondence, contact aaron.strickland@icloud.com. Contact your workspace operator about information held in Mantis, and Apple about information Apple holds.

In the UK, you can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

This page will be updated if Buglane's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
