CARRY ON: AIRPORT ARCADE

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how Carry On: Airport Arcade, published by Aaron Strickland in the United Kingdom, handles information when you play the game or contact support.

You can play without creating a game account. Game progress stays on your device. Apple handles purchases. When advertising is enabled, Google AdMob and its privacy services process information described below.

Your game progress

The app stores scores, level progress, Passport stamps, preferences and recent play statistics on your device. These records are used for game features and progress, and are not uploaded to a developer-run analytics service. Backups managed by Apple may include local app data.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional, one-time Remove Ads purchase. Carry On uses StoreKit to check your purchase entitlement, restore it and respond to refunds. The app does not receive your payment-card details. All games, levels and retries remain free.

Advertising and privacy choices

The ad-supported app uses Google AdMob for occasional ads between completed games, and Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices. Ads are not shown during gameplay or when you choose to retry.

Carry On disables Google's ad personalization and publisher first-party ID. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission to access the iOS advertising identifier. These settings do not prevent all data collection by Google's services.

Google may process your IP address and approximate location, app or device identifiers, advertising data, interactions, performance information and crash diagnostics. These support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Processing can involve Google and its advertising service providers.

Where available, open Settings → Ad Privacy to review or change your choices. Declining consent does not lock games or retries. Once Remove Ads is verified, the app stops loading and showing ads. It may still refresh privacy information to keep existing privacy choices accessible.

Google's retention practices, international processing and privacy controls are described in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Apple's services are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until removed with the app or its data. Restoring a device backup may restore these records. Apple manages purchase records independently, so deleting local progress does not cancel or delete your purchase entitlement.

Removing ads stops future ad loading; it does not erase records previously held by Apple or Google. Those providers manage their records according to their own policies and available privacy controls.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or including it in screenshots.

Your choices and rights

You can change game preferences in Settings and use the advertising privacy options when available. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, or withdraw consent. Contact Aaron about information handled for support; use Apple's and Google's privacy controls for information they hold. You can also contact your local data-protection authority.

These web pages

The Carry On support and privacy pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts. The website's hosting provider, Vercel, may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. See Vercel's Privacy Notice.

Contact

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, contact Aaron Strickland at aaron.strickland@icloud.com.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
