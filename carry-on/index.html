QUICK ANSWERS

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Open Settings from the home screen, choose Remove Ads, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. It is a one-time purchase; it is not a subscription.

Do I need to pay for levels or retries?

All four games, levels and unlimited retries are free to play. The optional Remove Ads purchase removes advertising.

Where is my progress saved?

Scores, levels, Passport stamps and preferences are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove that progress. Purchase restoration is managed separately by Apple.

How can I change sound, haptics or the background?

Tap the settings button on the home screen. Settings are also available while a game is paused.
