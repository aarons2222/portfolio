Before your next getaway

How do I play?

Choose a ride on the Home Screen and tap “Let’s get loose”. Swipe left or right to change lanes, up to jump and down to duck. The buttons at the bottom of the track work too. Match your height to the loot: jump for airborne prizes and stay low for items on the path.

How do I open the other worlds?

Complete the Escape goal in Willow Meadow (500 m), Sunset Coast (750 m) and Amber Orchard (1,000 m) to open the next world. Forager and High Flyer are optional goals. You can keep running after completing a goal.

Every world can be earned by playing. “Unlock all worlds” opens all four immediately and also removes ads. It is an optional one-time purchase: £4.99 in the UK. The app shows the current price for your App Store region.

Can I remove the ads?

Yes. Open the Home Screen’s three-dot menu and choose “Ad options”. “Remove ads” is a one-time purchase: £1.99 in the UK. It removes between-run ads; worlds can still be earned by playing. There is no subscription.

The £4.99 world pass already includes ad removal, so its owners do not need both purchases. If you buy Remove Ads first, unlocking worlds later is a separate purchase at the price shown in the app.

When are ads shown, and how do I change my privacy choices?

Goose uses occasional, limited Google ads at breaks after completed runs. There are no ads during gameplay, on launch or triggered by retrying a run. Your first app session is ad-free. An unavailable ad never stops you playing.

Where available, open “Ad options” in the Home Screen menu, then “Ad privacy choices” to review Google’s privacy form. Limited ads are not personalized, but Google still processes information to deliver ads and prevent fraud, and may use device storage. Goose does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open “Ad options” in the Home Screen menu, or the purchase section in the world selector, and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase. This restores eligible Remove Ads and world-pass purchases; it does not recover local scores or worlds earned by playing.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, best distances, earned world access and preferences are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove that progress. Apple manages purchases and device backups separately.

How do I change sound and haptics?

Open the vertical three-dot menu on the Home Screen. Sound and haptics have separate switches. Help is in the same menu.
