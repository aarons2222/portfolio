Goose on the Loose

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Goose on the Loose (“Goose”) is published by Aaron Strickland in the United Kingdom. This policy explains how information is handled when you play, visit these web pages or contact support.

You can play without an account. Game progress stays on your device. Google provides limited, contextual ads between runs. Optional one-time purchases remove ads or open all worlds with ads removed. Apple handles payments.

Information on your device

Goose stores game progress, best distances, worlds earned by playing, your chosen ride and your sound and haptics preferences on your device. These records provide the game’s features and are not sent to a developer-operated server.

Goose does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. Its 3D scenes do not use your camera. It does not require an email address or a game account.

Advertising and information handled by Google

Ad-supported play uses Google AdMob for occasional full-screen ads between completed runs. Ads do not interrupt a run, and retrying a run does not trigger an ad. Goose requests limited, contextual ads, disables ad personalization and Google’s publisher first-party ID, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or access to Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

Limited ads still involve data processing. Google may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device and app information, service identifiers where applicable, ad views and interactions, and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. This helps deliver and measure ads, protect against fraud and improve the advertising service. Goose does not include a separate third-party gameplay analytics service.

Ad software and creatives may be cached on your device. Google’s programmatic limited ads may also use cookies or local storage specifically to detect invalid traffic and prevent fraud. “Limited” does not mean no data is collected or no storage is used.

Google’s User Messaging Platform checks which privacy choices apply, presents any required privacy form and stores your choices. It may process IP-derived regional information, app interactions and performance information to provide this service. Google and any ad providers identified in the privacy form handle their information under their own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s explanation of limited ads.

Your advertising choices

Where Google requires a privacy choices entry point, open the Home Screen’s vertical three-dot menu, choose “Ad options”, then “Ad privacy choices”. Choices are handled separately from purchases. The game remains playable if an ad cannot load or privacy requirements prevent an ad request.

Consent is requested where required. Necessary delivery, security and fraud prevention may involve other lawful grounds, as explained in Google’s privacy information and the choices shown to you. Buying Remove Ads or the world pass stops future ad loading after Apple verifies the purchase; it does not erase information previously processed by Google. Existing privacy choices remain available where required.

Purchases handled by Apple

“Remove Ads” is an optional one-time purchase that removes between-run ads. The separate “Unlock all worlds” purchase opens all four worlds immediately and also removes ads. Every world can still be earned by playing. Neither purchase is a subscription.

Apple processes payments, and Goose uses StoreKit to check verified purchase entitlements, provide access, restore eligible purchases and respond to refunds or revoked access. If you own the world pass, you do not need to buy Remove Ads as well.

Aaron does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles Apple Account and payment information under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until the app or its data is removed. Delete the app to remove its local data. Offloading the app can retain data, and restoring an Apple-managed device backup may restore progress.

Deleting local data does not cancel a purchase or erase Apple’s purchase records. You can restore eligible purchased access using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple manages its records and any device backups separately.

Deleting the app does not delete information already held by Google. Google determines retention for information processed by its services; see Google’s information retention policy and its privacy controls for the options available to you.

If you contact support

If you email Aaron, your email address, message and any attachments are used to respond, investigate the issue and keep necessary support records. This processing serves the legitimate interest of providing support. Messages are handled through the support email service; they are not used for advertising or sold.

Correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary records or meet legal obligations. You can ask for deletion using the contact details below. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

These web pages

The Goose support and privacy pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or externally hosted fonts. They are hosted by Vercel, which may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. This serves the legitimate interest of operating a reliable website.

Google, hosting and email providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy information explains their processing and applicable safeguards. See the Google links above, Vercel’s Privacy Notice and Apple’s Privacy Policy for the services used here.

Your choices and rights

You can change game and advertising preferences in the Home Screen menu and remove local app data as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its use, withdraw consent, or request a copy. Contact Aaron about information handled for this app. Google and Apple manage requests about information held by their respective services.

You can also raise a concern with your local data-protection authority. In the United Kingdom, this is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children’s privacy

Goose is a general-audience game and does not ask children to create an account or provide personal details. Ads use the limited, contextual settings described above. If a child sends personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact Aaron to request its deletion.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Aaron Strickland at aaron.strickland@icloud.com.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
