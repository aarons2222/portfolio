PRIVACY POLICY

The Short Version

V0ID keeps your session history and preferences on your device. It has no V0ID accounts, advertising, personal analytics, tracking SDKs, cookies, or fingerprinting.

Data Stored on Your Device

V0ID stores session start and end times, planned and elapsed duration, completion status, lifetime totals, and app preferences locally. This data is used to show your history, resume an active session, update the widget, and remember your settings.

Purchase

V0ID offers one optional, non-consumable lifetime cosmetic unlock. Apple processes the purchase through StoreKit. V0ID can read whether that entitlement is active, but has no access to your payment information or Apple ID.

CloudKit

When iCloud is available, V0ID adds completed elapsed seconds to a shared aggregate CloudKit counter. It does not upload your session history, name, email address, or a V0ID profile. The local experience continues to work when iCloud is unavailable.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you enable the end chime, V0ID schedules a local completion notification. Live Activity and widget state are shared with the app's extensions on your device. V0ID does not send marketing notifications.

Third Parties

V0ID does not integrate third-party SDKs, analytics services, ad networks, or tracking tools. Apple services used by the app are subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

Data Retention

Local history remains on your device until you reset it in V0ID or remove the app. Contributions to the aggregate CloudKit total cannot be associated with a V0ID account or separated back into individual sessions.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy (we're not sure what you'd ask, but still), contact us at mail@aaronstrickland.dev.
