PRIVACY POLICY

The Short Version

V0ID does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No analytics. No tracking. No telemetry. No cookies. No fingerprinting. Nothing.

Subscriptions

Your subscription status is managed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We have no access to your payment information, Apple ID, or any other personal details.

CloudKit

The global subscriber counter uses Apple's CloudKit and contains no personally identifiable information — just a number going up (or down, we don't judge).

Third Parties

V0ID does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, ad networks, or tracking tools. There is nothing to integrate them into.

Data Retention

We retain exactly zero bytes of your data. There is nothing to delete because there was never anything to store.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy (we're not sure what you'd ask, but still), contact us at mail@aaronstrickland.dev.
