Support

Having an issue with V0ID? You've come to the right place. Although, to be fair, there's not much that can go wrong with nothing.

The app isn't doing anything.
That's correct. That's the entire point.
What is included for free?
The timer, session history, Live Activity, widget, Shortcuts, and optional end chime. The lifetime purchase only adds cosmetic void tones and pinned widget phrases.
Does the lifetime unlock renew?
No. Nothing Forever is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. You can restore it from the purchase screen.
I want a refund.
Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Try explaining to them that you want a refund for nothing.
Is my data being collected?
Session history and preferences are stored on your device. V0ID has no accounts, advertising, or personal analytics. When iCloud is available, only elapsed seconds are added to a shared aggregate counter.
Does V0ID work offline?
Yes. Sessions and history work offline. The shared aggregate counter updates only when iCloud is available.

Privacy Policy

V0ID stores session dates, durations, totals, and preferences on your device. It has no V0ID accounts, advertising, personal analytics, tracking SDKs, cookies, or fingerprinting.

The optional one-time purchase is handled by Apple through StoreKit. V0ID can read whether that entitlement is active, but has no access to your payment details or Apple ID.

When iCloud is available, completed time is added to a shared CloudKit total. No session history, name, email address, or V0ID profile is sent with that aggregate number.
