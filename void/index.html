How do I cancel my subscription?
Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → V0ID → Cancel. We won't try to guilt-trip you. The void doesn't care.
I want a refund.
Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Try explaining to them that you want a refund for nothing.
Is my data being collected?
No. V0ID collects nothing. Stores nothing. Tracks nothing. We don't even know you exist.

Does V0ID work offline?

Privacy Policy

V0ID does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No analytics. No tracking. No telemetry. No cookies. No fingerprinting. Nothing.

Your subscription status is managed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We have no access to your payment information, Apple ID, or any other personal details.

The global subscriber counter uses Apple's CloudKit and contains no personally identifiable information — just a number going up (or down, we don't judge).

That's it. That's the whole privacy policy.
