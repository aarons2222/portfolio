Wrecklet!

Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Wrecklet! is published by Aaron Strickland in the United Kingdom. This policy explains information handling in the game, on these pages and when you contact support.

You can play without a game account. Earned progress stays on your device. Apple handles optional outfit purchases and Game Center. Optional Google reward videos, where available, are off by default. Non-personalised advertising still involves data processing.

Game information on your device

Wrecklet saves scores, campaign and daily challenge progress, earned cosmetic coins and outfits, your equipped look, sound and haptics settings, and the optional-video preference in local app storage. Local reward records prevent the same round or video reward from paying twice. The game does not send these local records to a developer-operated server or include a separate gameplay analytics service.

The game does not require an email address or a developer account. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. After you choose to connect, Apple handles the Game Center identity and receives eligible completed-round scores and achievement progress for leaderboards and achievements. Wrecklet keeps app-scoped player attribution locally so results are associated with the correct Game Center player. Game Center does not synchronize earned cosmetic coins or outfits.

Outfit purchases and Apple

Optional paid outfits are permanent, non-consumable purchases. Wrecklet uses Apple’s StoreKit to obtain products and current prices, verify purchase entitlements, restore eligible outfits, and respond to refunds or revoked access. This includes purchase and transaction identifiers needed to provide the outfit. There are no subscriptions or purchased coin packs.

Apple handles Apple Account information, billing and payments. Aaron does not receive your payment-card details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for Apple services.

Optional Google reward videos

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where reward videos are available, you can enable them in Settings and then choose an offer after a round. Videos are off by default and are never required to continue playing. A completed reward supplies cosmetic coins, not a score or leaderboard advantage.

Wrecklet requests non-personalised advertisements, disables Google’s publisher first-party ID and the SDK’s crash-reporting feature, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or deliberately read Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA). These settings do not make Google’s services data-free.

Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device and app information or identifiers where applicable, ad views and interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. This supports ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention and service improvement. Ad software or creatives may use device storage. Disabling one identifier or crash-reporting option does not eliminate all service information.

UMP checks which privacy messages apply, presents required choices before ad requests and stores those choices. Google’s services may process information outside your country. Google explains its practices, retention and controls in its Privacy Policy, information for apps using Google services and retention information.

Video and privacy controls

Open Settings to turn Optional reward videos off. When the app’s advertising service is configured, Settings also provides Ad privacy choices. This refreshes the available Google privacy options; a form appears where required. The game remains playable when privacy requirements prevent an advertisement or no video is available.

Turning videos off stops future optional video preparation. It does not erase information previously processed by Google or replace the privacy choices shown by its service.

Retention, removal and restoration

Local game records remain in app storage until removed with the app or its data. Deleting the app can remove earned progress and preferences; offloading may retain them. Apple-managed device backups may separately preserve app data. Wrecklet does not provide a developer-hosted copy of earned progress.

Removing the app does not delete Apple’s purchase records or information already held by Google. Eligible paid outfits can be restored using the Apple Account that purchased them. Apple and Google manage their own records and retention.

Support messages

If you email support, Aaron receives your email address, message and any attachments to answer your request and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or verification codes. Contact the address below for questions about support correspondence or requests to access, correct or delete information Aaron holds.

For support correspondence, Aaron relies on the legitimate interests of answering requests, resolving faults and protecting the game and its players. Messages are handled through Apple’s iCloud Mail, not added to a marketing list. If keeping a record is required by law, that record is handled to meet the relevant legal obligation.

Support correspondence is retained only while needed to resolve and follow up the request, investigate a related fault or security issue, or meet an applicable legal requirement. Records are deleted when those purposes no longer require them; the period depends on the issue and any applicable obligation, rather than an indefinite support archive.

These web pages

The Wrecklet landing, support and privacy pages use local images and system fonts, with no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by these pages. The site is hosted by Vercel, which may process ordinary request information such as your IP address and browser information to deliver and protect the site. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice.

Necessary website delivery and security processing serves the legitimate interests of providing reliable public support and protecting the site against abuse. Hosting records are managed by Vercel under its service practices; no separate visitor profiles or analytics database are maintained for these pages.

General audience and contacting us

Wrecklet is a general-audience game. It does not ask children to create an account or submit personal details. A parent or guardian can contact Aaron about a child’s information sent to support.

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact Aaron Strickland at aaron.strickland@icloud.com. Depending on the information and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and ask for a portable copy where that right applies. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Use the game’s privacy choices for applicable Google consent controls. Apple and Google handle requests about information held by their own services through the controls linked above.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) or your local data protection authority. Contacting Aaron first may help resolve the issue, but is not a condition of making a complaint.

Changes

This page will be updated when Wrecklet’s information practices change. The date above identifies this revision.
