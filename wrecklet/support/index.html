Contact Aaron

Email aaron.strickland@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, the version shown in Wrecklet’s Settings, and a short description of the problem. A screenshot can help; hide personal details first.

Never send passwords, payment-card information or Apple verification codes. Purchase billing and refunds are handled by Apple.

Before your next mess

What is the objective?

Read the mission above Play. You may need to wreck a number of objects, earn points or reach a combo. You have 40 seconds. Once the goal is complete, tap BANK STARS to finish or keep wrecking for bonus points. Complete a stage to unlock the next one.

How do I move and shove?

Drag in the room to steer Wrecklet. Release to stop. On slippery bathroom tiles, steer early when changing direction. Tap the room or press SHOVE to hit nearby objects. A second finger can shove while you keep steering. Look for lines of clutter to trigger chain reactions.

What happens when I am caught?

Move out of the hand’s warning ring and the vacuum’s marked route. Each catch costs four seconds and breaks the current combo. You can replay immediately after the round.

How do coins and outfits work?

Finishing a round earns cosmetic coins for objects wrecked, reaching the mission goal and round stars. Classic Mischief is always yours. Mint Condition and Peachy Trouble use earned coins. Sunset Rascal, Starlight Sprite and Golden Gremlin are separate, permanent one-time purchases. There are no subscriptions or paid coin packs.

Open Shop from the house map, Settings or a round result. Tapping a card previews a look; it does not buy or equip it. Use Wear this to equip an owned look. Outfits change appearance only and never improve physics, scores, stars or leaderboards.

How do I restore a purchased outfit?

Open Shop, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Settings also has a Restore purchases action. Restoration brings back eligible paid outfits. It does not transfer coins or outfits earned on another device. Apple refunds or revoked access remove the corresponding paid outfit; an equipped unavailable outfit falls back to Classic.

For Apple billing and refund help, visit Apple purchase support.

The Shop says Unavailable. What should I do?

Check your connection, open Shop and tap Refresh shop. A purchase can start only after Apple returns its current product and price. The free missions and earned outfits remain available if the store cannot be reached. Contact support if the issue continues.

Do I have to watch advertisements?

No. Where reward videos are available, they are off by default. Enable Optional reward videos in Settings only if you want the choice. A ready offer after a round can give 30 cosmetic coins. Only a completed reward pays; closing early may not. No video is required to replay, and there are no banners or advertisements during gameplay.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Open Settings and choose Ad privacy choices when that entry is available. Google checks which form applies in your region. You can turn Optional reward videos off and keep playing. Non-personalised ads still involve information processing; see the privacy policy.

Where is my progress saved?

Scores, campaign and daily progress, earned coins and outfits, your chosen outfit and preferences are saved on this device. Earned progress is not synchronized through Game Center. Deleting the app can remove local progress. Apple handles purchases and device backups separately.

Do I need Game Center?

No. The game works without connecting. Game Center offers optional leaderboards and achievements. Connecting shares eligible completed-round results with Apple; it does not transfer local coins or outfit earnings.

How do I pause or change sound and haptics?

Use Pause during a round. Settings is available from the house map and the pause menu, with separate Sound and Haptics switches. Closing Settings keeps the round paused until you choose Resume. Moving the app into the background also pauses play.
