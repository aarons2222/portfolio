Small creature. Big mess.

WRECKLET!

A little gremlin. A house full of trouble.

Drag to scamper, tap to shove, and turn everyday clutter into a glorious chain reaction. Wrecklet has 40 seconds to make a mess before a very determined hand catches up.

How to play & get help →Your privacy choices

Native iPhone arcade · iOS 17 or later · Adapts to portrait, landscape and iPhone Duo displays

Make a chain

Send crockery and clutter into their neighbours, build a combo and dodge the hand. A completed mission earns stars; a bigger mess earns a better score.

Explore the house

Nine free missions across the Kitchen, Lounge and Bathroom, with a daily room mix for another little rampage.

Dress for mischief

Earn cosmetic coins by finishing rounds, preview new outfits, or choose a permanent extra look in the Shop. Outfits never change scores or stars.

Need a hand?

For help with the game, a purchase or an unexpected mess, email Aaron. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. Please leave passwords and payment details out of your message.

Email support →

Mischief on your terms

The missions and daily challenge are free. Optional purchases are permanent cosmetic outfits, with prices shown by Apple in the Shop. Where available, optional reward videos are off by default and can offer cosmetic coins after a round. Replay is always available without a video.
