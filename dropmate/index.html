Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Drop Mate Pro is published by Aaron Strickland in the United Kingdom. This policy describes how the app handles information and what happens when you contact support or visit this page.

Delivery information

Delivery stops, saved addresses, settings and photos are stored on your device. The app does not provide its own account or cloud synchronisation service.

Location, maps and photos

With your permission, the app uses your location for route planning, and uses the camera to scan addresses or take delivery photos. Addresses and locations are sent to Apple’s mapping services for geocoding and route calculations. When you choose an external navigation app, the destination is shared with that app; its privacy policy applies. You can control location, camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscriptions and payments. The app checks the subscription entitlement provided by Apple; it does not receive your payment-card details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

You can delete individual stops and saved addresses in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Device and iCloud backups are managed separately in Apple settings and may retain copies according to your backup settings.

Support and this page

If you email support, Aaron receives your email address and anything you choose to include, in order to respond. This page is hosted by Vercel, which may process technical request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security. See Vercel’s privacy notice.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including access, correction or deletion. Contact aaron.strickland@icloud.com. You can also contact your local data-protection authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.
